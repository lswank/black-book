9 frameworks

Pick a shape, get a prompt.

Each framework is a slash command. Pipe in what you want; get back a structured, copy-pastable prompt with the missing inputs surfaced.

T · A · G

/tag

Task · Action · Goal

Quick ad-hoc requests where you know what you want done.

B · A · B

/bab

Before · After · Bridge

Cold outreach, problem/solution copy, turnaround narratives.

R · T · F

/rtf

Role · Task · Format

Short, structured outputs — ad copy, headlines, social posts.

C · A · R · E

/care

Context · Action · Result · Example

Grounded work — landing pages, longer content with brand voice.

R · I · S · E

/rise

Role · Input · Steps · Expectation

Multi-step long outputs — full posts, scripts, sequences.

A · I · M

/aim

Action · Intent · Metric

Strategy — channels, OKRs, growth loops, market entry.

G · R · O

/gro

Goal · Reason · Output

Lightweight planning — newsletters, calendars, briefs.

F · I · T

/fit

Format · Input · Task

Transforms — press releases, meta tags, summaries.

L · E · D

/led

Level · Expectation · Direction

Calibration — when the same content could be written ten ways.

on demand

The prompt bank that doesn't go stale.

Static prompt libraries are a 2023 artifact — Claude already produces tailored prompts better than a 1,000-prompt PDF.

/prompt-bank linkedin post launching v2 of our SOC 2 product

→ Picks social-post-writer, applies R-T-F, asks for the missing audience and CTA, then emits the finished prompt and offers to run it.

15 specialists · v1 marketing

Specialists, not personas.

Two shapes: subagents that produce artifacts, and skills that load a thinking-partner persona into your current chat. Each declares which framework it's built on.

Subagents artifact producers

  • sales-email-writerR·I·S·ESales emails, sequences, follow-ups, re-engagement.
  • cold-outreach-copywriterB·A·BFirst-touch cold email and LinkedIn DMs.
  • landing-page-copywriterC·A·R·EFull landing pages or specific sections.
  • ad-copywriterR·T·FPaid ads — Meta, Google, LinkedIn, X, TikTok.
  • blog-post-writerR·I·S·ELong-form posts — guides, opinion, comparisons, listicles.
  • newsletter-writerG·R·ONewsletter editions with subject lines and structure.
  • social-post-writerR·T·FOrganic social — LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads.
  • video-script-writerR·I·S·EYouTube, shorts, sales videos, AI-video prompts.
  • press-release-writerF·I·TPress releases plus journalist pitch emails.
  • seo-meta-writerF·I·TTitle tags, meta descriptions, OG, schema.

Skills consultative thinking

  • cmo-advisorA·I·MStrategic marketing decisions — channel mix, brand, OKRs.
  • growth-strategistA·I·MGrowth loops, funnels, north-star metrics, experiments.
  • positioning-coachT·A·GPositioning, messaging, ICP, category, differentiation.
  • content-strategistG·R·OCalendars, pillars, distribution, repurposing chains.
  • competitive-analystC·A·R·ETeardowns, audits, market mapping, win/loss synthesis.
